THE SCIENCE

Biology meets
engineering.

AeronX combines biological carbon fixation with gas-flow engineering and environmental monitoring.

🌿 Photosynthetic carbon fixation

Microalgae use light energy to convert inorganic carbon into organic compounds through photosynthesis. Carbon is therefore incorporated into algal biomass.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

🌊 Gas–liquid mass transfer

CO₂ must first move from the gas phase into the liquid culture. Bubbling increases the gas–liquid contact area and can improve mass transfer.

💡 Light availability

Photosynthesis depends on available light. As algal biomass increases, light attenuation and self-shading can become important limitations inside the reactor.

🧪 pH & carbon chemistry

Dissolved CO₂ participates in the carbonate system of water. Changes in CO₂ concentration can therefore influence pH and the distribution of dissolved inorganic carbon.
